There are 10 flow careers per policy and 25 standard score careers per policy, sharing seeds across policies; successive seasons are not independent samples. Do not treat `14.12` as an observed average wait until eventual victory. Sources: `README.md`, `commands.sh`, `tools/career_score.gd:run/one`.

## Instrument audit

- **Checks that hold:** baseline flow matches bake-off #1's `flow_now.tsv` in every shared column. All 960 cash rows reconcile `previous bank + cc_in - cc_out = bank`, starting at 8 CC; no duplicate policy/base/seed/season keys. Sources: both flow files; `Season` initialization. This supports bookkeeping consistency, not whether every income mechanism is balanced.
- **`banked` is live but overpromises capacity.** `Career.levels_banked` walks XP bars, caps at nine, and treats `(potential - overall) × 4` stat room as a count of levels without simulating stat growth. The fixture's label 4 / actual manual capacity 1 disproves “placeable levels.” Allocation choice can affect ceiling crossing, so any replacement preview needs a declared priority, or wording that admits uncertainty. `unspent` has the separate eligibility defect above.
- **“After every event” is inaccurate.** `ProbeManager.season` places after `skip_event`, including training weeks, but not after each cup tie drained by its blocker loop. The real report starts a run only when an active-eight man is placeable; `FighterScene._all_order` then includes starters, travelling bench, reserves and injured men. Thus whole-roster spending is plausible once a run starts; unconditional spending after calendar steps and delayed cup spending are the mismatches. It is an engaged-manager scenario, not a measured average player or a strict upper bound.
- **These careers use aggregate-power `LeagueWorld.quick_bout`, not the detailed melee simulation.** Formation, specialist stat interactions and thumb skill are not directly exercised. C-3's mirror rate and C-2's orchestrated rate cannot turn these career rows into a proven floor for every engaged player. Oracle recruitment, omitted facility spending and different XP paths also break that claim. Sources: `SeasonBouts.skip_event`, `SeasonCups.sim_cup_tie`, `LeagueWorld.quick_bout`, `ProbeManager.value`.
- **Time and population boundaries matter.** Flow `power`, `tier`, `season_pts` are captured before rollover; its cash is after. The audit's final power is 95 before / 94 after rollover. Score `cc` averages balances *before* rollover, despite the README's end-of-season wording; score `power` is after. Winter `lv/lv_pts` exclude newly signed men; `lv_season` includes them. Neither flow stat-points column includes rollover training/ageing. XP earned, discarded or converted into CC is not measured directly. Do not add these columns and call the result total development. Sources: both probe implementations; audit `power_pre_rollover/power_post_rollover`.
- **Harness description understates reserves and omits wage exposure.** Actual `keep` includes `KITTY=20`; paid sessions and ceiling raises require another KITTY above that. Therefore “not behind KITTY” is false, although harness does precede those development purchases. `harness_cc` excludes wages and the starting-fixture upgrades do not measure every man's kit needs. Source: `ProbeManager.season`; README column definition.
- **`worlds` is neither Worlds wins nor distinct trophies.** `_record_honors` increments titles for cups/playoffs; `LeagueWorld.roll_over` increments again for the National playoff winner. The summary's `score_titles_counter` accurately names a counter, but a distinct-trophy interpretation still double-counts that championship. Do not use it to rank Worlds success.
- **Ceiling eligibility depends on kit.** With unchanged raw stats, the audit fixture moves from overall 68 / cannot place to 67 / can place solely by wearing the armor. Source: audit `KIT_FIXTURE`; `FighterCard.rating/effective_base`, `Career.at_ceiling`. Equipment can change level opportunity as well as finances; this data does not separate those causes of harness-policy slowdown.

## Design calls for Pete

1. Does the roughly ten-season championship target still mean the winter-only, oracle, quick-sim manager, or a player who follows the report's level prompts? These currently represent materially different pacing.
2. Is better harness intended as an early competing luxury, or foundational progression that should not postpone championships this much? The current harness-first policy is not evidence that every sensible purchaser suffers the same delay.
3. Should development eligibility use current worn-kit overall, or a stable underlying ability measure? Should auto clubs receive the same completed allocation as manual players near their ceiling? The stated flat-payment intent supports proposal 1, but CPU progression must still be checked.
